Preparation & Aftercare

Mandatory dermatological guidelines

Discontinue prescription retinoids (Tretinoin, Adapalene), AHA/BHA exfoliants, and active benzoyl peroxide formulations. Keep skin adequately hydrated using pure ceramide creams and avoid prolonged solar exposure.

Cleanse exclusively with lukewarm distilled or thermal spring water and non-foaming micellar washes. Apply prescribed lipid-recovery ointments twice daily. Avoid hot saunas, strenuous exertion, and abrasive facial towels.

Apply non-nano Zinc Oxide SPF 50+ broad-spectrum sunscreen every 3 hours during daylight. Mineral physical blockers prevent post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) while cellular restructuring occurs.
